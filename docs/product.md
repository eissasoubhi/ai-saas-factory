# Product strategy

## Positioning

AI SaaS Factory is not “another Next.js dashboard.” It sells avoided engineering work: production-grade account management, organizations, billing, entitlements, AI usage, mobile readiness and maintainable architecture.

## Target customers

- solo founders building B2B SaaS
- small product teams
- agencies repeatedly shipping client SaaS products
- AI product builders who need billing and multi-tenant foundations

## Differentiation

1. Web **and** mobile-ready monorepo.
2. Auth owned in-app rather than mandatory dependence on a hosted identity vendor.
3. First-class organizations, roles and entitlements.
4. Usage/credit metering for AI products.
5. Provider-neutral AI architecture.
6. Agent-friendly repo instructions and predictable package boundaries.
7. Tests around the failure-prone commercial plumbing.

## Initial pricing hypothesis

- Starter: €129 — one commercial project, web edition
- Pro: €249 — web + mobile, updates, advanced modules
- Agency: €599 — multiple client projects, broader redistribution rights subject to final license

Prices are hypotheses until the demo, documentation and support scope are complete.
